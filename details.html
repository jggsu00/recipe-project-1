Spaghetti Carbonara

A rich Roman classic made with eggs, Pecorino Romano, crispy guanciale, and cracked black pepper. Creamy without a drop of cream — it's all technique.

30 minServes 4 ItalianMedium
Ingredients
Suggested Next Steps
Step 1

Boil your pasta

Salt a large pot generously and cook spaghetti 1 min under al dente — it'll finish in the pan.

Confidence
98%
ⓘ Based on 4 servings and standard pasta timing.
Step 2

Render the guanciale

Cook on medium heat until fat is translucent and edges crisp. No oil needed.

Confidence
91%
ⓘ Confidence based on typical fat-render time at medium heat.
Step 3

Temper the egg mix

Whisk yolks, egg, and cheese. Add a ladle of pasta water slowly before combining with pasta.

Confidence
87%
ⓘ Lower confidence — tempering is technique-sensitive.
Step 4

Toss off heat

Remove pan from heat before adding egg mixture. Toss fast — residual heat does the work.

Confidence
94%
ⓘ Off-heat finish prevents scrambling — high reliability.

Honey Garlic Salmon

Pan-seared salmon glazed with honey, garlic, and soy sauce. Flaky, caramelized, and on the table in under 20 minutes.

20 minServes 2 SeafoodEasy
Ingredients
Suggested Next Steps
Step 1

Pat the salmon dry

Drying the surface is the key to a good sear. Press with paper towels for at least 30 seconds per side.

Confidence
96%
ⓘ Dry surface = better Maillard reaction. High confidence.
Step 2

Make the glaze

Whisk honey, soy, garlic, and lemon in a small bowl before you start cooking.

Confidence
99%
ⓘ Simple ratio — very reliable.
Step 3

Sear skin-side first

Hot skillet, 4 mins skin down without moving. It will release when ready — don't force it.

Confidence
88%
ⓘ Timing varies slightly by fillet thickness.
Step 4

Add glaze & baste

Flip, pour glaze over, and spoon back on top every 30 seconds for 2 minutes until caramelized.

Confidence
92%
ⓘ Caramelization depends on pan temp — medium-high recommended.

Classic Beef Tacos

Seasoned ground beef piled into warm tortillas with shredded cheese, fresh salsa, and a squeeze of lime.

25 minServes 4 MexicanEasy
Ingredients
Suggested Next Steps
Step 1

Season the beef

Mix cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, and salt together before adding to the pan.

Confidence
99%
ⓘ Standard taco seasoning ratio — very reliable.
Step 2

Brown the meat

Cook over medium-high, breaking it apart. Don't rush — let it brown, not steam.

Confidence
93%
ⓘ Browning improves flavor significantly. Based on typical pan size.
Step 3

Warm the tortillas

30 seconds per side on a dry skillet, or wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave 45 seconds.

Confidence
97%
ⓘ Both methods tested — high reliability.
Step 4

Assemble & serve

Beef first, then cheese so it melts slightly, then cold toppings. Lime goes on last.

Confidence
95%
ⓘ Order of assembly affects texture and temperature balance.

Chicken Tikka Masala

Tender chicken in a bold, spiced tomato-cream sauce. Best served over basmati rice or scooped up with warm naan.

45 minServes 4 IndianMedium
Ingredients
Suggested Next Steps
Step 1

Marinate the chicken

Mix yogurt, half the spices, salt, and chicken. Marinate at least 1 hour — overnight is best.

Confidence
85%
ⓘ Longer marination = deeper flavor. Lower confidence for short marination.
Step 2

Char the chicken

Grill or broil on high to get some char. This smokiness is what makes tikka tikka.

Confidence
90%
ⓘ Char level depends on your specific heat source.
Step 3

Build the masala

Saute onion until golden, add garlic and ginger, then spices, then tomatoes. Simmer 15 mins.

Confidence
94%
ⓘ Bloom spices in fat before liquid for best flavor extraction.
Step 4

Combine & finish

Add charred chicken and cream. Simmer 10 mins. Taste and adjust salt. Garnish with cilantro.

Confidence
96%
ⓘ Final simmer melds all flavors — don't skip it.
