Our Mission

We are Jason Lopez and John Gomez, the creators of Golden Pantry. Jason is an undergraduate student at Georgia State University, and John is a graduate student at Georgia State. As Hispanic culinary artists who love bold and spicy flavors, we created Golden Pantry to share our passion for cooking and bring people together through food. We built Golden Pantry to share delicious recipes and inspire home cooks to create amazing meals in their own kitchens. From pasta to tacos and everything in between, we enjoy experimenting with different ingredients and adding our own creative twists to dishes we love. Our goal is to help people discover new flavors, learn new cooking techniques, and feel confident trying new recipes. We also want Golden Pantry to be a community where others can share their own favorite recipes, ideas, and traditions so everyone can learn from each other and enjoy great food together.
